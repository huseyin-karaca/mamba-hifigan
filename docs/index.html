Mamba HiFi-GAN

Integrating Bidirectional Mamba into HiFi-GAN for Long-Range Speech Synthesis

Hüseyin Karaca, Rabia Ela Ünlü, Emre Can Yayla

Bilkent University

PaperCodeSlides

Abstract

High-fidelity waveform generation requires modeling both fine-grained periodic texture and long-range prosodic structure. While HiFi-GAN achieves strong results through multi-receptive field dilated convolutions, its bounded receptive field limits global temporal modeling, potentially causing inconsistencies in longer utterances. To address this without the quadratic computational cost of Transformers, we integrate Mamba, a selective State Space Model (SSM) with linear complexity, into the HiFi-GAN generator. We introduce a Bidirectional Mamba block to capture global context in non-causal synthesis and evaluate multiple architectural configurations through a systematic ablation study. Our best hybrid architecture, which employs an adaptive gated fusion mechanism to dynamically balance convolutional texture modeling and SSM-based global structure, achieves a 15% improvement in perceptual quality (Feature Matching Loss) over the baseline while maintaining competitive spectral fidelity.

Method

Each upsampling stage runs the Bidirectional Mamba block beside the MRF module, on the same features. A learnable scalar wi per stage mixes the two:

αi = σ(wi),   y(i) = αi ΦMamba + (1 − αi) ΨCNN

wi starts at 0, an even split, and training sets the balance at each resolution.
