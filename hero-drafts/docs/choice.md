# Choice between draft A and draft B (hero2 revision)
Both drafts share the top strip, the stage card, the fusion and the gate; they differ in the two lane glyphs the user called "too simple and bad". Renders: figure/out/hero2.png (A), figure/out/hero2_b.png (B).

**A: scan streams + nested funnels.** What an art editor would flag: the MRF window is drawn at about 60% of the drawn axis, so it is not to scale (the caption must say so); some empty space at the lower left of the MRF lane; the lane-shade meaning ("how much of the sequence the state has absorbed") needs the caption.
**B: discrete state cells + window bars.** Flags: the Mamba chain of 30 small cells with arrows reads as the old "boxes in a row" glyph the user rejected; the tiny arrows between cells vanish at 640 px; the MRF window bars plus dot taps look like a bar chart, not a convolution; the "mean" routing doglegs across the lane.

**Picked A**: fewer and smaller problems, and the stronger contrast (one continuous stream across the whole axis against three bounded funnels), which is the takeaway.
