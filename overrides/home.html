CS 585 term project · Bilkent University · Fall 2025

Mamba HiFi-GAN

Adaptive gated fusion of convolutional and bidirectional-SSM branches in a neural vocoder

Hüseyin Karaca, Rabia Ela Ünlü, Emre Can Yayla

Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Bilkent University

HiFi-GAN's convolutions model speech texture well but see only a bounded window. We tried nineteen ways of giving the generator a bidirectional state-space branch as well, and measured all of them. Most made things worse, in three distinct ways. The one that helped mixes the two branches with a weight the network learns for itself.
